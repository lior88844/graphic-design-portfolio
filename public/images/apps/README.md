# Apps images

Drop your app screenshots / photos in this folder.

Expected filenames (used by the site):

- `hero.png` — large image shown on the card and at the top of the Apps detail page
- `1.png`, `2.png`, `3.png`, ... — gallery images shown in the grid

The card preview on the Work page uses `1.png`, `2.png`, and `hero.png`.
You can use `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.jpeg` — just keep the names consistent with
`src/content/projects.ts`.
